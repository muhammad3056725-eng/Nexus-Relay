Name the guest exactly to trigger the 15-second Overclock: name things connected to them to multiply your score up to ×2.5.

        
Scoring: BASE = 1,000 × (guesses left ÷ 10), plus a closeness bonus up to 250 for warm misses, all × your Overclock multiplier. The deep stuff — five-axis telemetry and the relay deck — lives in the other DETAILS tabs, for when you want the instruments.
